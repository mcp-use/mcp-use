# AI contribution policy

AI tools may help you contribute, but the human contributor is responsible for the submission and its maintenance.

- Review and understand every change you submit. Be prepared to explain the implementation, tradeoffs, and tests, and respond to review feedback yourself.
- Disclose material AI assistance in the issue or pull request, including which tools you used and what they helped produce. Do not submit unverified claims, fabricated results, or generated text you have not checked.
- Test the change yourself. Report the exact commands or manual steps and their results; explicitly state what you could not test and why.
- Keep contributions focused on a real, documented problem. Do not submit speculative issues, bulk-generated reports, or large unrelated changes. Check for duplicates first.

All contributions must also follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Maintainers may close submissions that do not meet these requirements.
